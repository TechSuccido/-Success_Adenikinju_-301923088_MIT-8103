# Case Study: ShopSphere (simulated multi-vendor e-commerce platform)

- **Organisation / scenario:** ShopSphere, a simulated online marketplace operating in Nigeria.
- **Nature of the system:** Customers browse products from many independent sellers, place orders, pay, and track delivery. Sellers manage listings and stock.
- **Users of the database:** customers, sellers, warehouse/logistics staff, finance/admin staff, data analysts.
- **Major data stored:** customers and addresses, sellers, categories, products, inventory, orders and order items, payments, shipments, reviews.
- **Key operations supported:** add products, place orders, update stock, process payments, track delivery, search the catalogue, produce sales reports.

This same case study is used in all five portfolio activities.
